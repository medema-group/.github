Transferring GitHub repositories
======

This step-by-step manual will guide you in transferring your repository to the Medema-Group GitHub organization. 

1. Ask Marnix to add you as a member to the Medema-Group
2. Go to the **Settings** tab of your repository
3. Navigate to the bottom of the **Settings**, to the **Danger Zone**
4. Click on **Transfer ownership**
5. At **New owner**, click on **Select one of my organizations**, and select `medema-group`
6. Confirm and transfer
7. Ask Marnix to give you `maintainer` or `administrator` rights with the transferred repository
